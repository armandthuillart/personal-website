In recent months, I have been advocating to anyone I come across, that they should write. Not only should they write, they should publish their writing online for the purpose of sharing it openly with as many people as possible.

The biggest benefit of writing, is that it allows you to store and organize your feelings, thoughts and ideas in a coherent manner as opposed to keeping them all jumbled in your own mind.

Because in your head, ideas feel complete. They feel obvious, even profound. Then you try to put them into words and you realize you only had the outline of something, not the thing itself. Writing makes you close that gap. It makes you figure out what you actually believe.

But the part that surprised me most was how much it works as a record. When you write down what you are going through, what you are thinking, what you are afraid of or excited about, you are leaving something for yourself. A version of you that is more honest than memory, because memory edits. Memory softens the hard parts and exaggerates the good ones. Writing just says what was true on that particular day.

And when you go back and read it, sometimes months later, sometimes years, it is one of the strangest and most clarifying experiences I know. You see how far you have come. You see patterns you could not see from inside them. You understand yourself in a way that is hard to get any other way.

It is more healing than most things people reach for when they are trying to feel better. And more powerful than it looks from the outside.

So write the things you want to be written. Document everything. You will be glad you did.
