Writing a post is one job. Publishing it everywhere you want is another. The work of moving the same idea from one platform to the next can take more time than it should.

[Crosspost](https://github.com/armandthuillart/crosspost) is a tool I built to help people write social media posts in their own voice and publish across platforms in one go.

## Keep the writing personal

The phrase “in your own voice” matters to me. A useful tool should help with the work around writing and publishing while keeping the post recognizably yours. Crosspost brings the work into one flow, from shaping a post to sharing it where you want it to appear.

## Spend less time repeating the same steps

Each platform has its own audience, but the effort of copying a post between apps is rarely the valuable part. Crosspost is built around a simple idea: write what you want to say, then make publishing it to your platforms easier.

It is a focused project about a common task. I want software to remove busywork and leave the important choice with the person using it: what to say, and how to say it.
